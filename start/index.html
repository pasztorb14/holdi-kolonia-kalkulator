Our Vision

This platform is just the first step. We want to build a 3D simulator that is realistic both scientifically and visually, where anyone can experience what it might be like to live on the Moon and found a colony there.

Countless people have ideas but no way to prove them. We want to give everyone the opportunity to test their own and show it to the wide World.
